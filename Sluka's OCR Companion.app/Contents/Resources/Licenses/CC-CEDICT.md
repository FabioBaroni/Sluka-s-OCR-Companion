# CC-CEDICT data

The bundled Simplified and Traditional Chinese to English dictionary comes from CC-CEDICT, published by MDBG and maintained by CC-CEDICT contributors. It is the unmodified October 14, 2026 snapshot also distributed by LongYan Reader. CC-CEDICT continues the CEDICT work of Paul Andrew Denisowski (1997, 1998).
